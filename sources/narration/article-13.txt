SUNDAY, MARCH 21, 1926

FUTURE OF SIX FATHERLESS TOTS, WHOSE DADDIES GAVE LIVES TO CITY, DEPENDS ON RELIEF DRIVE.

How Will Kalamazoo Reward Firemen’s Supreme Devotion to Duty; Question Asked in Campaign.

In two modest Kalamazoo homes, one in Douglas Avenue and the other in Lumm Avenue, a pall of sorrow has enveloped two bereaved mothers and six fatherless little tots—made fatherless by their daddies, John Morren and Claus Slater, who gave their lives fighting the First Methodist Church fire a week ago.

Hearts of a sympathizing community have gone out to these grief-stricken families, and the people of Kalamazoo and many surrounding cities have paid tribute to the memory of the two brave firemen who were crushed beneath falling roofs and walls. Funeral rites have been conducted for the firemen, and the sorrowing families have returned to homes shrouded in sadness and fatherless forever.

WILL KALAMAZOO AID?
But will Kalamazoo sit by and permit the widowed mothers to carry on alone in the care and protection of their six fatherless children?

This is the question the Underwriters’ Club of Kalamazoo is asking residents of the community. It is a question which the club believes can be answered best by means of contributions to a fund which is now being raised for the care and education of the little children who are destined to pass through their most tender years without the kindly aid and guidance of a father.
