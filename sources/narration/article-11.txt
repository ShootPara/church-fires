SUNDAY, JUNE 13, 1926

MEMORIAL WILL BE HELD FOR FIREMEN
RELIEF ASSOCIATION TO HOLD SERVICES THIS MORNING AT 9:00

List of deceased members, the Firemen's Relief Association of Kalamazoo will observe its annual memorial services at 9:00 Sunday forenoon at the grave of Claus Slater, one of the two firemen killed in the First Methodist Church fire last winter.

The Rev. Emanuel Rushbook, chaplain of the Kalamazoo association, will have charge of the services. There will be a sermon and appropriate music. Graves of the dead firemen will be decorated.
