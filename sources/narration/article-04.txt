SATURDAY, APRIL 10, 1926

METHODIST CHURCH TO BE TORN DOWN
PLANS BEING DRAWN FOR NEW EDIFICE AT ACADEMY AND PARK STREETS

Demolition of the ruins of the First Methodist Church structure at Lovell and Rose streets is to be started at once. Trustees of the church have advertised for bids. C.A. Krill, chairman of the board, says no steps will be taken toward the erection of the new church until the return of the Rev. D. Stanley Coors, pastor. He is now in the West, but is expected home early in May.

Ernest Batterson, local architect, is preparing preliminary sketches for the new church, which will be erected at the Academy and Park Street site. Mr. Krill reports minor differences pertaining to fire insurance have been worked out and adjusted, and that a settlement of the insurance will follow in a few days.
