RECOVERED IN TWO HOURS
It was nearly two hours before the bodies of the dead firemen were finally dug from the ruins. The rescuers worked in shifts.

Two Kalamazoo firemen were killed, and five others were injured Saturday evening in a fire which swept the First Methodist Church at Rose and Lovell Streets to complete ruin. The loss is estimated at $100,000.

The firemen met death when they were caught beneath a falling balcony at the north end of the church. The balcony fell in as the south side of the big brick tower gave way. Tons of brick and heavy beams came down upon the heads of the firemen almost without warning. Those who escaped barely managed to reach the front vestibule door. Others were caught underneath the wreckage and were helpless until rescued by fellow firemen.

As the three men were holding the line of hose, others, who had moved about six feet to one side, heard a crash above and noticed the roof of the second balcony—about 40 feet in the air—give way. They called to their companions to "run for your lives" and dashed to the door. Leyden, thought to have been the nearest to the door, just crossed the threshold as the falling brick and timbers crashed to the floor. Kruizenga and one or two other firemen also reached safety, but not before falling brick had struck their feet.

Slater and Morren did not reach the door. Both men had gotten within a foot of safety when their bodies were carried through the floor to a depth of two feet. They both fell within a few inches of each other and were undoubtedly crushed to death.
