WEDNESDAY, JULY 9, 1926.

PRESBYTERIANS WILL MEET AT H.S. AUDITORIUM
Officials Continue Probe of Fire Which Destroyed Edifice.

First Presbyterian church services for the next year, at least, will be held in the Central high school auditorium, it was announced Friday, following a conference of church trustees with the board of education.

The Sunday services, July 11, will be held at 11:00 in the forenoon, in the auditorium. The union services in the evening will be conducted at the First Baptist church.

MEET WITH BOARD.

A committee composed of Dr. J. T. Upjohn and George Irwin conferred with the board of education on behalf of the church trustees and made arrangements for use of the church. The Presbyterian board believes it will be fully a year before a new church will be near completion.

Sunday school at the Presbyterian church has been discontinued for the summer and there are no meetings now of the young people's society.

The board of trustees met Thursday afternoon with officials of a firm of architects in Chicago for the purpose of going over the plans for the new church building and completing arrangements for starting the work on the new structure. The plans soon will be completed, it was announced.

PROBE CONTINUES.

No new developments in the two investigations being conducted into the Presbyterian church fire were reported Friday by the sheriff's and police departments nor by Deputy State Fire Marshal Frank Burmaster, who is working with two men from the state fire marshal’s office.

The investigation is to continue, however, and will cover not only the Presbyterian church fire but also the several other church and lodge hall fires which have occurred in Kalamazoo during the last six months.
