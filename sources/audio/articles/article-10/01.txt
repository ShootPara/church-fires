WEDNESDAY, MARCH 17, 1926

BURY JOHN MORREN WITH FULL HONORS
CITY PAYS TRIBUTE TO SECOND FIREMAN WHO DIED IN CHURCH BLAZE.

Honor was paid to the memory of John Morren, fireman, second victim of the First Methodist Church fire Saturday night, at funeral services at 2:00 Wednesday from the Third Christian Reformed Church, Park and Walnut Streets.

Kalamazoo people paused again Wednesday afternoon to pay a last tribute to a man who gave his life in the performance of his duty as a fireman and to express sympathy to the widowed mother and three children.

City and county delegations, club and lodge representations, and visiting Metropolitan clubs from several Michigan cities that attended the funeral services of Claus Slater, companion of Morren and a victim of the church fire, Tuesday afternoon, were present at the Morren services. The city hall remained closed from 12:00 to 4:00 in honor of the man, and Mayor George K. Taylor and City Manager Ross J. Buck headed the delegation of city officials and employees that attended the funeral.

The Rev. John P. Battema, pastor of the Third Christian Reformed Church, officiated at the Morren rites. There was prayer and music.

The body of John Morren had an escort of firemen and policemen as guards of honor, the same as the Slater remains. These guards formed around the casket in the church and marched with it to the funeral car. The body was escorted from the funeral car to the grave by these guards.

The funeral parade today was composed of virtually the same organizations that marched in memory of Claus Slater yesterday. City firemen were present in a body and led delegations from upward of seven different Michigan cities. About two hundred firemen and policemen appeared in line.

Although the Morren services were not to begin until 2:00, the church was filled to capacity by 1:30, as was the case at the Slater funeral in the First Baptist Church Tuesday. Scores of people were obliged to wait out-of-doors in the cold at the Slater funeral.
