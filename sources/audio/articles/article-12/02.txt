The tragedy of last Saturday night was one of the worst shocks which has come to Kalamazoo in a long time. A wave of sincere, genuine human sympathy literally swept the entire city. Now there is a splendid chance to translate that sympathy into something tangible, something truly expressive of the way Kalamazoo feels toward men in its public service who do not hesitate to make the supreme sacrifice when duty calls. The people cannot hope to compensate these children for the loss of their brave fathers. That is not the point. But the people can pay at least a part of their debt to Claus Slater and John Morren; they can, if they will, build up a fund to assure these children of the chance in life which men of such character as their fathers would have given them. That is fair enough, isn't it?
