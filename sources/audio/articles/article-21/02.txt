Two of the disastrous fires—the First Methodist church and the Trades and Labor Council hall, occurred about 5:00 Saturday afternoon.

SOME OCCUR ON SUNDAYS

Three of the fires—the East Avenue M. E. church, the Herfendahl store and the Boudenman block, occurred Sunday forenoons.

The First Congregational church and the Moose temple fires occurred around the noon hour.

The Chase block fire was the only early-morning blaze.

The Phythian building fires were all in the late afternoon.

All the fires which were discovered in the First Presbyterian church occurred in the late evening, usually around 11:00.

The peculiar circumstances of these fires are about the only facts on which the officers have to conduct their investigation. A rigid inspection of the charred ruins of the Presbyterian church was being made Wednesday.
