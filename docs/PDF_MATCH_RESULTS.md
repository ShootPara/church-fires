## Observed corrections and limits

Photo crops, corner decorations, continuation markers, transparent text backgrounds, comparison arrows, layered imagery and model posters were restored. Slide 73 now uses eleven crops of Brian's named full-page article. Slide 53's automatically swapped source assignments were corrected by visual review.

The approved brick/cream palette, Arial text, centered paper panels and single modern bottom control row remain intentional differences from the PDF. Original tiny navigation and speaker icons are omitted. Exact font metrics and some decorative image-mask contours differ from the PDF. This review establishes composition and source identity, not pixel equality or newspaper transcription accuracy.

Brian's name is removed from visible slides and the continuous-reading copy. No narration-pending or unfinished-work labels appear. Future narration must follow original slide assignments. Slide 1 has none.
