## Main findings

The assets are substantially present, but the builder loses the PDF's visual hierarchy. Every image gets one equal grid cell (up to four columns), so a tiny date strip competes with a headline or a full photograph. Native text becomes a generic heading and paragraph stream; church identifiers and continuation labels can become page titles. A permanent header/title band consumes space absent from many original slides. Missing arrows, white text boxes, overlapping images, rotated labels and unequal column arrangements explain much of the remaining mismatch.

The stylesheet also caps many images/videos at 350px and allows the main material area to scroll. Those choices prevent reliable slide fit. Correct composition and fit before transcribing more newspaper text or generating audio.

- Check actual fullscreen entry/exit, Escape, resizing, keyboard arrows, chapter/page selectors, visible focus, article overlay close/focus return and media pause-on-navigation. Inspect the longest available transcript for internal scrolling. One focused pass plus reruns of failures is sufficient.
